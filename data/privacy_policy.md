# Company Privacy Policy
- We collect name, email, delivery address and order history.
- Customers can ask to access, correct or delete their data (a data subject request, DSR).
- We respond to every DSR within 30 days.
- Order data is kept for 3 years, then deleted. Invoices are kept for 7 years because tax law requires it.
- We never sell customer data.
- Contact: privacy@company.example
